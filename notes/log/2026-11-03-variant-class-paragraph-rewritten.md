Previous paragraph:

  Each variant class contributed differently to four-class phasing (Fig.~\ref{fig:methylation}f, Supplementary Fig.~4e--h and Supplementary Table~6). Adding 5mC to SNVs increased block N50 by 2--16\% at 10--60$\times$ and kept SNV switch error rates within 3\% of those of SNV-only phasing. Adding indels increased N50 by 16--33\% but also increased switch error rates by 14--24\%. Adding SVs changed N50 by at most 1.7\%, because an SV does not join two blocks on its own. With all four classes, blocks were 18--46\% longer than with SNVs only (4.14 versus 2.87~Mb at 60$\times$), with 14--27\% higher SNV switch error rates. Adding SVs and 5mC to indels changed indel-pair switch error rates by at most 7\% (Supplementary Table~2). In four-class phasing, \toolname phased 97.4--99.2\% of the heterozygous 5mC sites that it called, 66.1--68.5\% of the heterozygous SVs called by Sniffles2 and 30.5--45.4\% of benchmark heterozygous indels (replicate~1; Supplementary Data~1).
