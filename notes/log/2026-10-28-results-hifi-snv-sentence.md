# Results: HiFi SNV-only rates given per tool in one sentence, 2026-10-28 (author decision, option A)

In the HiFi paragraph of `sections/results.tex`, the sentence "Rates decreased from 0.049% to
0.033% for LongPhase 2, from 0.085% to 0.057% for HiPhase, …" described the change across
coverage within each tool. It was removed, and the preceding sentence now gives each tool's
rate range in parentheses, followed by the fold reductions (1.7–1.9, 2.4–2.8 and
2.9–3.5-fold), in the same structure as the nanopore SNV-only sentence. Rates decrease
monotonically for all four tools (Supplementary Table 10), so the ranges equal the 10× and
50× values.
