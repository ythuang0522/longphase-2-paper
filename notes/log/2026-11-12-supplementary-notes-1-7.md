Nature-family journals, and "Method 6" (benchmark data, tool configurations and evaluation) is not a
method of LongPhase 2. All supplementary text sections are now one series under the heading
"Supplementary Notes".

`figures-source/make_results_figs.py`, `figures-source/supp_tables.tex`,
