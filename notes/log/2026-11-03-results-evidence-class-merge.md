Previous text:

Each evidence class increased contiguity (Fig.~\ref{fig:methylation}f, Supplementary Fig.~15e--h and Supplementary Table~9). Adding 5mC kept SNV switch error rates within 3\% of SNV-only phasing and increased N50 by 2\% at 10$\times$ and 8--16\% from 18$\times$. Indels increased N50 by 16--33\% and switch error rates by 14--24\%. Four-class phasing produced blocks 18--46\% longer than SNV-only blocks (4.14 versus 2.87~Mb at 60$\times$), with 14--27\% higher SNV switch error rates. Its blocks were within 1.7\% of those of SNV, indel and 5mC phasing without SVs, which were longer at every coverage except 30 and 40$\times$, because an SV does not join two blocks on its own. Adding SVs and 5mC to indels changed indel-pair switch error rates by at most 7\% (Supplementary Table~13).

The phased fraction varied by variant class in four-class phasing: \toolname phased 30.5--45.4\% of benchmark heterozygous indels, 97.4--99.2\% of the heterozygous 5mC sites that it called and 66.1--68.5\% of the heterozygous SVs called by Sniffles2 (replicate~1; Supplementary Data~1).
