LongPhase 2 manuscript wording review

Review date: 9 October 2026. Source revision: bd5490a. Review only; manuscript sources and figures were not changed.

The strongest problem is the supplementary text's mixture of scientific methods, software documentation, internal review instructions and tutorial-style explanations. The main text is more controlled, but the Introduction still contains broad claims and rhetorical phrasing. Supplementary Fig. 17 retains stronger claims inside its raster image than the current Results support.

“AI-like” here describes an editorial impression, not evidence of authorship. Technical detail is not itself a defect. Keep details needed to reproduce the method or interpret the results. Remove repeated explanation, author-facing instructions, unused implementation options and claims that exceed the evidence.

Coverage: abstract; Introduction; Results; Discussion; main Methods and availability statements; all six main legends; Supplementary Methods 1–6; Supplementary Notes 1–2; Supplementary Tables 1–15 and Data 1 description; all 17 supplementary legends. Compiled PDF text was checked for visible notes and figure labels. Supplementary Fig. 17 was read visually because its text is embedded in a raster image. This is an editorial review, not a new numerical, code, citation or full-layout audit. Percent-prefixed LaTeX comments are excluded from the findings about published prose.
