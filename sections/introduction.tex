Haplotype phasing, the assignment of heterozygous variants to their parental chromosomes, is essential for interpreting the diploid human genome. Determining whether two variants lie in \textit{cis} or in \textit{trans} is central to identifying compound heterozygosity in recessive disease\cite{eisfeldt2025clinical}. Allele-specific methylation on a phased haplotype reveals its parent of origin\cite{akbari2023parentoforigin}. Long-read sequencing has made read-based phasing practical at clinical and population scale. Individual reads span tens to hundreds of kilobases, link distant heterozygous sites directly and carry native base-modification signals; a single library thus yields haplotype blocks orders of magnitude longer than short reads allow\cite{logsdon2020longread,kolmogorov2023scalable}. Read-based phasing does not require parental sequencing, a population reference panel of the kind statistical phasing depends on\cite{hofmeister2023shapeit5}, or the additional Hi-C or Strand-seq libraries used to phase whole chromosomes\cite{porubsky2021fully}.

Read-based phasers assign heterozygous alleles to haplotypes by maximizing agreement with the reads, and they differ in both their models and the variant types they phase. \whatshap solves a weighted minimum-error-correction problem\cite{patterson2015whatshap} and, by re-aligning each read to both alleles, phases indels, including large ones, together with single-nucleotide variants (SNVs)\cite{martin2016whatshap}. \hapcut uses a likelihood-based model and also phases indels\cite{edge2017hapcut2}, Margin phases SNVs with a hidden Markov model\cite{shafin2021margin}, and HiPhase jointly phases small variants, structural variants (SVs) and tandem repeats from PacBio HiFi reads\cite{holt2024hiphase}. We previously introduced \longphase\cite{lin2022longphase}, which recasts phasing as a weighted allele graph, resolves haplotypes at chromosome scale and co-phases SNVs with SVs\cite{smolka2024sniffles2,jiang2020cutesv}. Each phaser therefore handles a different subset of variant types, and none phases sequence variants and allele-specific methylation in one model. Read-based phasing is no longer an end point but an intermediate step whose output other tools use directly. Clair3, ClairS and ClairS-TO phase reads with \whatshap or with \longphase as part of variant calling\cite{zheng2022clair3,zheng2026accelerated,zheng2026clairs,chen2025clairsto}; the EPI2ME wf-human-variation workflow phases every nanopore genome with \longphase\cite{ont2025wfhuman}; and Sniffles2 and Severus call SVs from the haplotagged alignments that these phasers write\cite{smolka2024sniffles2,keskus2025severus}. A phase error therefore does not stay inside the phaser: a single switch reverses the haplotype labels of every read beyond it in the block, and the small-variant, somatic and SV callers that rely on those labels carry the error into their own output.

Methylation offers a further source of phase evidence, but methylation-aware phasers gain contiguity at a possible cost in accuracy. Haplotype-specific CpG methylation (5-methylcytosine, 5mC), which long reads report together with the sequence, can link reads across regions that lack heterozygous sequence variants\cite{akbari2021nanomethphase,fu2024methphaser}. \methphaser, HapBridge and LongHap use this signal to join blocks after, or alongside, sequence-based phasing\cite{fu2024methphaser,chen2025hapbridge,pfennig2026longhap}; \methphaser, for example, lengthened phase-block N50 by 78--151\% at a reported phasing accuracy of 83.4--98.7\%\cite{fu2024methphaser}. Because the informative CpGs are learned from reads that the sequence phase has already assigned, its errors propagate into the methylation evidence. Methylation is also a less consistent marker than a sequence allele: haplotype-specific methylation is confined to a small fraction of CpGs, and the state of a CpG can differ between molecules of the same haplotype, varies between cells and tissues, and is called as a per-molecule probability rather than a base\cite{akbari2021nanomethphase,fu2024methphaser}. A block join that rests on a few such CpGs can reverse the phase of every variant beyond it; methylation therefore needs to be weighed against the sequence alleles on the same reads, not added after the phase is fixed.

Phase errors are not spread evenly across the genome. Base-calling errors cluster in homopolymers and tandem repeats, reads misplaced in segmental duplications create spurious linkages between heterozygous sites, and heterozygous calls in copy-number-altered regions can mislead the phasing graph\cite{olson2023benchmarking,dwarshuis2024stratifications}. Current phasers weight read observations by base quality\cite{patterson2015whatshap,edge2017hapcut2}, but a high-quality base on a misplaced read still supports a false linkage. Phasers also give little indication of which phase assignments are wrong. \whatshap and Margin report no per-variant confidence, and \longphase reports only read support. \hapcut prunes variants with low posterior confidence\cite{edge2017hapcut2}, but misplaced reads that agree with each other can keep this confidence high. Learning-based phasers learn to optimize the phasing objective, not to detect its errors\cite{xue2022neurhap,battistella2025ralphi}. A phaser therefore needs both to weight evidence by its sequence context and to mark the phase assignments that remain unreliable.

Phasers are commonly benchmarked against Genome in a Bottle (GIAB) truth sets built largely by mapping reads to GRCh38\cite{zook2019giab,wagner2022giab}. The HG002 v4.2.1 release excludes both sex chromosomes and 12\% of the autosomes, mostly satellite arrays and segmental duplications in which mis-mapped reads and collapsed paralogues create false linkages\cite{wagner2022giab,hansen2026q100,olson2023benchmarking,dwarshuis2024stratifications}. It also phases only the 74\% of heterozygous variants on which trio-based and Strand-seq phasing agreed\cite{wagner2022giab}. Expanding GIAB from 85\% to 92\% of GRCh38 revealed eightfold more false negatives in a short-read call set\cite{wagner2022giab}. The recent complete diploid assembly of HG002, T2T-HG002, phases every heterozygous site on both haplotypes of every chromosome, including 701~Mb of autosomal sequence absent from v4.2.1\cite{hansen2026q100}. It therefore allows phasing to be assessed in the difficult regions that mapping-based benchmarks exclude.

Here we present \toolname, a read-based phaser that addresses these limitations. It phases SNVs, small indels, SVs and allele-specific 5mC in one weighted graph, so that methylation is weighed against the sequence alleles during haplotype inference. It down-weights read evidence in difficult sequence contexts, and a graph neural network withholds phase assignments that are likely to be wrong. We evaluate it on nanopore and PacBio HiFi data against both the mapping-based and the assembly-based HG002 benchmarks.
